## Why AppleSupport

The brand has enough support volume to support a held-out evaluation and meaningful historical-resolution retrieval. Its conversations also contain multi-turn troubleshooting patterns, device/software context, private-channel handoffs, and common issue families such as battery, updates, apps, account access, connectivity, hardware, purchases, and repairs.
